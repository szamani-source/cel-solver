celFoam user manual

celFoam is an open-source explicit Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) solver for large-deformation solids and bulk materials: silo and hopper discharge, column collapse, footings, piles and projectiles in soil. It is built on OpenFOAM v2512 and released under GPL-3.0.

What celFoam does

A CEL solver combines two descriptions of motion. Material that flows a long way, such as sand leaving a silo, is tracked on a fixed Eulerian mesh: the material moves through the cells, and each cell records how full it is. Structures that keep their shape, such as a footing, a pile or a steel projectile, are Lagrangian bodies that move through the fixed mesh. Forces pass between the two. This is the same idea as Abaqus/Explicit CEL and LS-DYNA multi-material ALE Benson 1992 Qiu et al. 2011.

celFoam integrates the equations explicitly in time. Each step:

  1. picks a stable time step from the elastic wave speed and the smallest cell (CFL condition);
  2. updates the stress in every cell with the material model;
  3. computes the forces between bodies and material on the cells the bodies cut;
  4. moves the material and the bodies (central-difference velocity and position update);
  5. maps the moved material back onto the fixed mesh (remap);
  6. writes results when asked.

Capabilities

AreaWhat is available
Eulerian solidExplicit cell-centred finite volume, Lagrange step plus remap, material and void in every cell, free surface, plane strain (2D), 3D and the axisymmetric wedge
Material modelsElastic, von Mises (J2), Drucker-Prager, Mohr-Coulomb with tension cut-off, rate-dependent friction \(\mu(I)\), a nonlocal friction law with a length scale, cohesion, and a compaction cap
BoundariesSmooth and frictional walls, wall adhesion, outlets that open at a set time, a solid inlet that feeds material in, walls from STL surfaces
MeshesAny OpenFOAM hex mesh, including wall-aligned blockMesh meshes with angled cells of varying shape
Lagrangian bodiesRigid bodies from STL files (prescribed or free motion), deformable bodies of hex8 elements with hourglass control
CouplingForces in both directions between bodies and the Eulerian material, through the cells each body's surface cuts (no penalty stiffness)
RunningSerial or MPI parallel on any number of processors, restart from any written time

OpenFOAM supplies the mesh, the fields, the parallel decomposition and the file input and output Weller et al. 1998. The physics lives in four libraries of our own: celConstitutive (material models), celEulerian (the Eulerian solid), celLagrangian (bodies) and celCoupling (the cells bodies cut). OpenFOAM's own source is never changed, so any standard OpenFOAM v2512 installation can host celFoam.

Conventions

  • SI units throughout: metres, kilograms, seconds, pascals.
  • Stress is positive in tension. The pressure is \(p = -\tfrac13 \operatorname{tr}\boldsymbol\sigma\), positive in compression.
  • Bold symbols are vectors and tensors, for example the velocity \(\mathbf v\) and the Cauchy stress \(\boldsymbol\sigma\).
  • Angles in input files are in degrees.
  • Commands to type are shown in boxes like this one:
source /usr/lib/openfoam/openfoam2512/etc/bashrc
./Allwmake
