# celFoam 1.0.0

First release of celFoam, an explicit Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) solver for large
deformation of solids and bulk granular material, in the spirit of Abaqus/Explicit CEL,
built on OpenFOAM v2512 (openfoam.com). GPL-3.0.

**Install:** see `INSTALL.md`. Any Linux: build against OpenFOAM v2512 by hand or with
`./install.sh`; or `./install.sh --container` with Docker or Podman. On Ubuntu 24.04,
`./install.sh --get-openfoam` installs OpenFOAM too. Then `celfoam test`.

**Manual:** `docs/manual/index.html` (installation, quick start, case setup reference,
meshing, theory, validation, FAQ), also `celfoam manual`.

## What is in it

- **Eulerian solid** (celEulerian): explicit cell-centred finite volume, Lagrange step
  plus remap, material plus void with a geometric free surface, per-material history,
  outlets that open at a set time, solid inlets, gravity ramp.
- **Materials** (celConstitutive): linear elastic, von Mises, Mohr-Coulomb and
  Drucker-Prager (compression or plane-strain fit), with cohesion, dilation angle,
  tension cut-off, mu(I) rate-dependent friction, nonlocal fluidity (a length scale
  for shear bands), a compaction cap, and resting-state relaxation to K0.
- **Walls and bodies** (celLagrangian, celCoupling): walls from STL files (immersed,
  with friction and adhesion), rigid bodies (fixed, moving or free), deformable hex8
  finite-element bodies with hourglass control, penalty contact both ways.
- **Meshes:** Cartesian meshes, and wall-aligned (body-fitted) hex meshes with angled
  and graded cells, as blockMesh makes; axisymmetric wedge; STL walls inside either.
- **Running:** MPI parallel (with bodies), exact restart and checkpoints with
  `writeFormat binary`.
- **Tutorials:** smokeBox, siloDischarge, siloCohesion, siloFed, stlTank, stlSilo,
  stlHopper, rigidBodyMotion, and **coutoHopper**, the validation case below.

## Known limitations

These are open and documented; results near them should be read with care.

1. **Hopper wall at rest reads high.** GA0 on the Couto hopper wall at rest is 36 %
   above the measured range. The gravity-on start has no filling history; filling from
   the top with `restRelax` brings GA0 into range but overloads the upper cylinder by
   25-60 % (decisions 0108, 0114, 0115). GA1 in discharge is 12-17 % below Couto's
   maximum.
2. **Mesh dependence of shear bands.** Local friction laws localise into bands one to
   four cells wide. The nonlocal law (`nonlocalA`) brings the interior velocity field
   within 5-10 % between meshes, but column-collapse run-out still grows with refinement
   because the free-surface front rules set it (decisions 0038, 0111). Keep the cells
   that carry the shear band at 10-20 grain diameters and check a second mesh.
3. **Compaction and filling.** The cap model compacts material, but stresses locked in
   by impact during top filling are not relaxed without `restRelax`, and the realistic
   filled state (heap, packing density) is not reproduced (decisions 0113-0115).
4. **Quasi-static loads on frictional material are upper estimates.** A numerical rate
   effect (about rho c v) adds resistance in slow pushes: the sand footing reads 55 to
   160 % high depending on push speed. The low-damping rework (A.5) is parked
   (decisions 0095, 0096). Clay (Tresca) footings and piles are within 2-12 %.
5. **Small time step on fine meshes.** The explicit step is set by the smallest cell; on
   the 4 mm Couto mesh the axis cells at the outlet give dt 3.5e-6 s, so 1.5 s of
   silo takes about 8 h on 4 cores. Mass scaling and a wider axis column are later work.
6. **Parallel runs** match serial to round-off only until a switch (cap, void rule)
   flips; outcomes then differ like a serial run with a 1e-12 perturbation (decision 0080).
7. **The free surface (`freeSurface yes`) needs hex meshes** (Cartesian or wall-aligned
   blockMesh); general polyhedral meshes are not supported.
8. **Material values in most tutorials are placeholders** (mu(I) constants, grain size),
   except the coutoHopper wheat.
9. **Not in this version:** gas in the void and fluid coupling (planned for version 2),
   temperature, rate-dependent metal plasticity (Johnson-Cook), a case-setup GUI.

The full list is under "Known issues" in `docs/STATUS.md` and on the manual's FAQ page.
